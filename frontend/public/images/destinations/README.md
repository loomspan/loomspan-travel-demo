# Destination imagery

The three `960 × 640` WebP images are original AI-generated editorial travel illustrations made with the built-in Codex image generation tool on 2026-09-24 for this DeTour demo. Prompts requested recognizable landmarks for San Francisco (Golden Gate Bridge), Munich (Marienplatz), and Mexico City (Palacio de Bellas Artes), with wide landscape framing, natural light, and no text, logos, or watermarks. They are project-owned assets, not photographs copied from the design references. Source PNGs were resized and encoded to WebP at quality 82 for local delivery.
